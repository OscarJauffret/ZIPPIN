        Above Normal, ZIPPIN re-checks every change against the live timetable and shows connections the official search hides because it assumes a slower walker. The floor is the real gap between arriving and the onward train leaving.

        ⚠️ ZIPPIN mode allows zero-minute changes. A train leaving the same minute you arrive is not catchable unless it is across the same platform — and often not even then. Each one is labelled, and every result tells you what to do if you miss it.

        Free travel inside the night window means you only buy the part of the trip before it opens. ZIPPIN reads the stop sequence and tells you which station to buy to.

        Timetable data from transport.opendata.ch. Prices and purchases always happen on sbb.ch — ZIPPIN never handles fares or payment.

        Independent hobby project built on public open data. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to SBB CFF FFS. Live delays are not applied; always confirm tight changes on the platform display.
